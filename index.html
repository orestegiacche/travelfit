AMRAP 20 Min

  • 400m Runx1
  • Plank Up-downs20

Plank up-downs: start in a high plank, lower one arm at a time to forearm plank, then press back up. Alternating arms each rep.

▼

  • Min 1 — Jumping Squats12
  • Min 2 — Burpees12
  • Min 3 — HSPU or Pike Push-ups12

Scale Options

The Descender
⏱ ~15 Min 🎒 Bodyweight Medium
▼

For Time

  • Sit-ups60
  • Lunges (alternating)50
  • Push-ups40
  • Single-leg Squats or Air Squats30
  • Burpees20

A descending chipper — reps go down as movements get harder. Work through the list top to bottom. Single-leg squats if you have them, air squats if not.

Strategy

Don't sprint the sit-ups — you'll blow up on the lunges. Treat the first three movements as a build, then empty the tank on the burpees.
