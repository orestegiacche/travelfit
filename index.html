HSPU Setup

Find a solid wall — stone walls in Italy are everywhere. Kick up, hands ~6–8 inches from the wall, shoulder-width or slightly wider. Lower slowly, press explosively.

Leg strength, pulling, core compression, and conditioning all in one. The L-sit is harder than it looks — tuck your knees if 15s straight is too much.

L-sit on the Floor

Use two fists (knuckles down). Press hard, lock elbows, lift hips, extend legs. Even 5 seconds unbroken is solid progress.

Amalfi Ladder
25–35 min Ascending Ladder Bar or DB
▼

Ladder 2-4-6-8-10... — Pull-up Bar

  • Pull-upsladder
  • Handstand Push-upsladder ÷ 2
  • Burpeesladder
  • 200m Runevery round

Ladder 2-4-6-8-10... — Dumbbell

  • DB Thrustersladder
  • Tuck Jumpsladder
  • Burpeesladder
  • 200m Runevery round

HSPU ladder is half the pull-up count (round of 4 = 2 HSPUs). Keeps them achievable as the ladder climbs. 25-min cap, aim to finish the round of 10.

Strategy

Rounds 1–4 feel easy — that's the trap. Break HSPUs into singles by round 8 if needed; missing a rep and re-kicking up wastes precious time.
